# Don's QOL v0.5.0 release notes

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-06

Everyone playing together needs the same version, so update as a group. Your settings, chest categories and favourites carry over.

**Back up your world and character first.** Worn gear and moving items between bases are new and change how items are stored, and these features have had limited play so far.

### Added

- **Character menu.** A panel below the inventory shows a live 3D view of your character, which you can drag to turn. Helmet, chest, legs, cape, utility and trinket move into their own slots and free their inventory spaces. Weapon, shield and ammo slots show what is equipped. Empty slots show an item outline, and a held piece of gear lights the slot it fits.
- **Compendium.** A spoiler-free, searchable, cross-linked wiki in the character menu that fills in as you discover items, creatures, biomes and building pieces. It reads the game's own data, so other mods' additions are included.
- **Base management.** A window in the Don's QOL menu lists every base in the world, wherever you stand, with biome, chests, fullness and distance. Unnamed bases get a name automatically, and you can rename any of them.
- **Move items between any two bases.** In Base management, press **From** on one base and **To** on another, read the preview and confirm. Items go to the destination's chests by category. Items the world's portal rule blocks stay where they are, and so does anything that doesn't fit. The preview and summary count both.
- **Quick put away.** Shift + right-click an inventory stack to send it straight to the base's chests by category.
- **Put away selected.** Press the button in the Don's QOL menu, click the items you want, and press it again to move only those. The button shows how many stacks are picked, **Cancel** clears the selection, and equipped items never move.
- **Chest previews.** Each chest's row in the Don's QOL menu shows icons of its most plentiful items with a thin fill bar.

### Changed

- Every panel the mod builds now uses the game's wood, so the menus match the inventory.
- The Don's QOL menu groups its buttons by what they act on, with **Close** and **Base management** in the top corner.
- Favourite stars moved to the top-left of the slot, so they no longer cover stack counts.
- Lists scroll much faster.

### Known limits

- **Take worn gear off before uninstalling.** Without the mod, the game keeps gear worn and saved but has no slot to show it or take it off from, until the mod is back.
- A far base's chest count can read low, because station upgrades that extend range aren't counted when it's far away. Those outer chests can be left out of a move.
- Base management, taking gear off and turning the character view need a mouse.
- The preview counts stacks with no room but doesn't name them, and it doesn't say when items land in a chest of another category because their own is full.
- The worn slots assume the normal four inventory rows, so a character with extra rows may conflict.
